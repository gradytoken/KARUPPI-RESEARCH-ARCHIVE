## 2. KARUPPI relevance criteria

Prioritise books that may teach us about:
1. Remote Tamil village life and collective behaviour.
2. Mountain, forest, estate, shrine and old-house geography.
3. Local restrictions, ritual behaviour and the social enforcement of belief.
4. Oral memory, conflicting testimony, concealed histories and physical evidence.
5. Temple / sculpture / iconography / Siddhar traditions where concrete cultural material is present.
6. Mystery engineering: clue chains, delayed explanations, audience knowledge and emotional residue.

A title or genre tag alone is not enough. Do not infer plot from words such as *Naagam*, *Vaasal*, *Vanam*, *Ragasiyam* or *Marmam*.

## 9. Project boundary

- Catalogue descriptions are discovery aids, not substitutes for reading.
- Publisher / bookseller synopsis is weaker evidence than the actual text.
- Fictional Siddhar, temple or ritual descriptions are not proof of real cultural practice.
- Extract craft mechanisms, not plot, characters, signature images or supernatural devices.
- This catalogue screen does **not** authorise new KARUPPI mythology, a replacement historical Story B, or causal explanations for existing 1990 mysteries.
- Expand the screen when the remaining pages become accessible or are shared as screenshots / title lists.
