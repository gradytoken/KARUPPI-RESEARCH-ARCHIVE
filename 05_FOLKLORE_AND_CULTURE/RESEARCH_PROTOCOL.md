# Folklore and Culture: Research Protocol

## Principle

Do not treat "Tamil folklore" as one uniform system. Record the locality, community, language register, historical period, source type, and the source's own framing.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer, where available:
1. Primary local testimony or documented practice with clear provenance.
2. Scholarly ethnography / history / folklore research.
3. Archival records and regional publications.
4. Reputable journalism with named sources.
5. Popular retellings, blogs, and social posts as leads only.

A source's age does not automatically make it reliable. A contemporary source does not automatically make it representative.

## Fiction safeguards

- Do not present an invented ritual as authentic regional custom.
- Do not combine unrelated traditions merely because they look visually compatible.
- Distinguish living religious practice from fictional horror imagery.
- Avoid using a community as a monolithic "primitive" chorus.
- Preserve uncertainty when sources disagree.
- If a detail is invented for the film, label it as fictional world-building.
