# ISR-005 — Conditional Transfer Note for KARUPPI

**Source:** *Aranmanai Ragasiyam* (supplied 74-page scan, approximately 144 printed pages)  
**Status:** Craft transfer only. No story element is canonised by this note.

## Strongest transferable mechanisms

### 1. Mundane pressure before mystery

Parani's debt, low wages and desire for wealth establish a human want before Banarasidas introduces the uncanny. A mystery gains grip when it enters a life that already has pressure.

**KARUPPI test:** Does the current investigator have an immediate, ordinary pressure that makes continuing the investigation costly or necessary? Do not add a new pressure just because Parani has one.

### 2. A physical image can alter identity

The photograph connecting Banarasidas's appearance to the old Irulan gives a visual clue a character must confront. It changes how Nadachivam reads the present visitor.

**KARUPPI test:** Could an existing drawing, photograph or family object force a re-evaluation? Use only evidence already available in the current-day story bible, and do not decide that two mysteries connect until the story proves it.

### 3. A place boundary is stronger when socially enforced

Murugan's refusal to continue towards Naga Karadu creates a boundary before Parani crosses it. The audience understands that the danger is shared local knowledge, not merely the protagonist's imagination.

**KARUPPI test:** Existing village restrictions should be visible in people's routes, work, speech and refusal. No new forbidden place is approved by this note.

### 4. A physical object can carry an old obligation

The Naga Ratnam links an earlier broken promise to the present. The object stores moral history, not just supernatural power.

**KARUPPI test:** Treat existing objects as evidence only where their provenance, access and consequences can be established. Do not import reincarnation or gem-based karma.

### 5. Geography can contradict memory

The final empty hill challenges Parani's memory of the sanctuary. A physical location can become an evidence test: what is there now, what was there before, and who else can verify it?

**KARUPPI test:** Any mismatch between remembered and observed geography must change the character's available choices or confidence. It cannot simply erase prior events.

## Risks to avoid

- Too many independent lore systems competing for the audience's attention.
- Long explanations that deliver the mystery instead of letting the protagonist test it.
- A protagonist who becomes an audience surrogate while other characters do all the discovering.
- A coma / hallucination ending that makes earlier events feel disposable.
- Treating fictional claims about Siddhars, snakes, gemstones or rituals as cultural evidence.

## Exclusions

Do not borrow the Naga Ratnam, Irulan, past-life plot, Banarasidas identity, doll-guarded town, Naga Karadu, giant cobra, coral Devi, Sri Chakra activation, Naga Siddhar, Ashtama Siddhis, or world-palace concept.

The 1990 KARUPPI mystery remains unchanged. Story B stays removed. No replacement explanation is approved.
