# ISR-006 — Conditional Transfer Note for KARUPPI

**Source:** *Kallukkul Pugundha Uyir*  
**Status:** Craft transfer only. No story element is canonised by this note.

## Why this source matters

This novel gives a sacred sculpture a social life. Nandi is not merely a temple prop. Within the story, it is connected to agricultural custom, harvest, the village's identity, the protection of land and the moral limits imposed on outsiders. The novel's central miracle is fictional, but the community role built around the sculpture is a useful craft study.

## Transferable mechanisms

### 1. An object should belong to a functioning social system

The Vidaithangi Nandi is connected to farming, temple share, village memory and local behaviour. People act differently because the object matters to the community.

**KARUPPI test:** Does an existing shrine, object or place in the 1990 village affect daily behaviour, work, access or social standing? Check the Master Story Bible before proposing any new function.

### 2. A repeated physical detail can bridge story strands

The injured bull's leg recurs after Shiva Prakash's road incident. That image keeps the earlier offence present in later scenes.

**KARUPPI test:** Could an already-established object or image carry a trace of an earlier action into a later scene? The trace must be evidentially plausible and change what a character believes or does.

### 3. Show the local rule through behaviour

The one-petition custom, the bull's protected status and the warning not to publicise the Nandi event are communicated through what Mami, Velayudham and Varadhan say and do.

**KARUPPI test:** Demonstrate existing village restrictions through refusal, route changes, silence, access and social consequence before explaining their origin.

### 4. Let commercial power collide with local custom

Shiva Prakash sees land as a business opportunity and the Nandi secret as a publicity opportunity. The village sees the bull, temple and fields as part of an inherited sacred order.

**KARUPPI test:** Where an existing mystery already involves family property, gold, access or local authority, examine the competing incentives. Do not create a new land conflict merely because this book has one.

### 5. Control the convergence of multiple strands

The book brings paralysis, Nandana's danger, the injured bull, land acquisition, the Nandi legend and the rare animation into one sacred-centre explanation. This is an instructive structure and a warning: too many strands can dilute the protagonist's agency.

**KARUPPI test:** Every strand must alter the central investigation or a character's choices. If a strand only adds lore, consider whether it is necessary.

## Risks to avoid

- Long folklore / temple-history lectures that interrupt the present-tense conflict.
- A protagonist who only receives explanations from elders or specialists.
- A miracle that conveniently resolves multiple unrelated problems without sufficient earlier evidence.
- Treating a novel's fictional ritual rules as verified religious or historical facts.
- Turning a sacred object into a decorative symbol with no social consequence.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not transfer the walking Nandi, 32-lakshana count, 108-year cycle, broken-mark mechanism, Devadasan, Pallava-era origin story, mantra-animated sculpture, Vidaithangi village system, Nandana's actress / gangster plot, Naganatha's paralysis, or the miraculous recovery mechanism.

The current-day 1990 KARUPPI mystery remains unchanged. Story B stays removed. No replacement mythology or causal explanation is approved.
