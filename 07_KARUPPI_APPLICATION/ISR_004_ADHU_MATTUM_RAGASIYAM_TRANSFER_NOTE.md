# ISR-004 — Conditional Transfer Note for KARUPPI

**Source:** *Adhu Mattum Ragasiyam*  
**Status:** Craft application only. No story element is canonised by this note.

## Why this source is relevant

The novel's central prohibition initially appears to be religious fear. Its eventual function is to conceal a land-grant inscription that undermines the landlord family's authority. The reveal matters because it changes the material and social status of an entire community.

This is useful as a craft question for KARUPPI: could an established village behaviour have a practical, institutional, or historical function that the protagonist initially misreads? This is only a question. It does not authorise a land deed, estate plot, or replacement mythology for KARUPPI.

## Transferable mechanisms

1. **Rule before explanation.** Let the audience observe a rule being obeyed, enforced, and feared before learning its origin.
2. **Behaviour reveals power.** Show who can enter a place, ask a question, travel, speak, receive information, or refuse an order.
3. **Clues alter choices.** An object earns its place when it changes a route, risk, relationship, or decision.
4. **Oral testimony is partial.** A witness may preserve an event while misunderstanding the motive or missing the part they were not allowed to see.
5. **A reveal can change a social system.** The ending can expose the mechanism that kept a community trapped, not merely identify one culprit.

## Tests before any application

- Does the mechanism solve a specific existing KARUPPI mystery, or is it being imported because it worked in another book?
- Is the proposed cause supported by the current Master Story Bible?
- Would the reveal change character choices and community behaviour?
- Could the audience infer part of the answer from earlier evidence?
- Does the mechanism preserve the current 1990 story's established identity?

## Explicit exclusions

Do not transfer the source's locked temple, temple bell / deity rule, snake-pit keys, underwater chest, idol, land-grant inscription, hereditary Oozhiyampadi labour system, Puliyan, Varadamalai, or Samadhana Pandi conspiracy.

The 1990 village elements already recorded in the KARUPPI project remain separate. Story B stays removed. No replacement explanation has been approved.
