# KARUPPI: Current-Day Mystery Preservation Map

**Timeline:** 1990  
**Purpose:** Preserve established mysteries and track evidence without prematurely explaining them.

## Known story elements

| Element | Current working status | Investigation discipline |
|---|---|---|
| Isolated Tamil village / mountain and forest geography | Established setting context | Map routes, sightlines, distances, and community access when supported by the story bible |
| Darkness begins around 3 PM | Core mystery element | Track when it occurs, who observes it, what changes, and what evidence is available |
| Village restrictions and unusual behaviour | Established atmosphere / social system | Record who enforces each restriction and what happens when it is challenged |
| Nightly torch procession into forest | Established recurring event | Track participants, route, absences, timing, and what is directly witnessed |
| Abandoned shrine, eternal fire, faceless statues | Established mystery imagery | Keep observation separate from interpretation |
| Arjun disappears | Inciting / investigation event | Record last confirmed sighting and evidence only when present in the master story bible |
| Rajan investigates | Protagonist function | Preserve agency and track what each discovery changes |
| Thangam | Important character / investigation partner | Define exact knowledge and relationship only from approved story material |
| Selvi's drawings | Clue-bearing material | Track each drawing and what it changes; do not assume its source or meaning |
| Monthly gold transaction / gold sack | Mystery thread | Track dates, people, physical evidence, and who knows what |
| Velanthirayar family secrets | Mystery thread | Do not assume a link to the other threads without evidence |
| Kandasamy's death | Mystery / consequence | Preserve confirmed facts separately from village interpretations |
| Old family painting, basement, burned anklet | Potential evidence / mystery objects | Record exact appearances and consequences from the master story bible |
