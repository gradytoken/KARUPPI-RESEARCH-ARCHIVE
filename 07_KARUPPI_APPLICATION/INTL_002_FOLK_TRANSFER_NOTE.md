# INTL-002 — Folk: Conditional KARUPPI Transfer Note

**Source study:** 02_BOOK_STUDIES/INTL_002_Folk.md  
**Source:** Zoe Gilbert, *Folk* (Bloomsbury electronic edition, 2018)  
**Status:** Research application only. No story canon changes approved.  
**Project boundary:** Preserve the current-day 1990 village mystery. The historical Story B mythology is removed from active development. Do not reintroduce it through research analogies.

## 1. Why this book matters to the project

*Folk* demonstrates how a fictional community can become coherent through shared geography, recurring characters, work, customs, family ties, gossip, seasonal time and accumulated memory. Its most useful contribution is not a particular creature or ritual. It is a method for making the community itself carry story information.

The book is a work of fiction inspired in part by the Isle of Man. It is not ethnographic evidence and does not authenticate any custom for a Tamil village.

## 2. Candidate techniques to test against the current Master Story Bible

| Technique from the study | KARUPPI test | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Social knowledge network | For each relevant resident, separate firsthand observation, inherited belief, rumour, inference and deliberate concealment. | Do not assume villagers share one belief or know the same facts. |
| Operational geography | Audit existing places and routes for visibility, sound, access, travel, weather, work and social ownership. | Use only locations established in the current Master Story Bible. Do not invent connecting tunnels, secret routes or geography. |
| Ritual as social system | For an already-established custom, document who performs it, who participates, who is excluded, the public explanation and the actual evidence. | Do not add a ritual or reveal a hidden function without approval. |
| Event residue | Track how an established event affects later speech, behaviour, objects, memory and relationships. | A repeated mention does not prove a causal connection between separate mysteries. |
| Limited witness knowledge | Distinguish what a child or partial witness saw from what they inferred and what the audience can verify. | Do not turn a character's mistaken explanation into objective supernatural fact. |
| Sensory recurrence | Audit established sounds, smells, light and textures across scenes so a return can change meaning through context. | Sensory repetition is not proof of a supernatural link. |
| Community pressure | Test how status, fear, jealousy, family loyalty and gossip affect testimony. | Do not use collective belief as a substitute for evidence or character motivation. |
| Circular motif | If an approved procession or action recurs, test whether changed context deepens its meaning. | Do not create a secret origin or new mythology for an existing action. |

## 3. Current-day material to preserve, not reinterpret

The repository's current status document records the following active or working elements: the 1990 timeline; unnatural early darkness; village restrictions and behaviours; a nightly torch procession entering the forest; an abandoned shrine with an eternal fire and faceless statues; Arjun's disappearance; Rajan's investigation with Thangam; Selvi's drawings; a monthly gold transaction / gold sack; Velanthirayar family secrets; Kandasamy's death; and several objects or places whose exact functions require checking against the latest Master Story Bible.

This is a preservation reminder, not an invitation to connect those items. The exact causal relationships remain subject to the current story bible and explicit creative decisions.

## 4. Non-transfer list

Do not import the Gorse Mother, water-bull, Jack Frost, Redwing, changeling explanations, Ox-man initiation, Old Merry's witch story, kite-skin flight, fungi or Neverness-specific customs into KARUPPI. Do not present them as Tamil folklore. Do not revive the removed Karuppi / Maari historical Story B, the Ravi-Selvaraj triangle, counter-spell or magical-ring lineage.

## 5. Recommendation

Use *Folk* as a community-construction and narrative-echo reference. The practical next step is a source-controlled audit of the existing KARUPPI village map, witness knowledge and established rituals against the latest Master Story Bible. This should remain a separate research exercise. No new plot explanation is authorised by this note.

**Decision:** Conditional techniques recorded. No canon changes. No new mythology. No causal links inferred.
