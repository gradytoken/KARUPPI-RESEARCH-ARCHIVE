# UNTITLED GUARDIAN PROJECT — Research Archive

A version-controlled, evidence-led research archive supporting an animated Tamil folklore-horror mystery film. The active working identifier is **UNTITLED GUARDIAN PROJECT**.

## Project identity and naming

- **Temporary working identifier:** UNTITLED GUARDIAN PROJECT.
- **Retired active titles:** KARUPPI: THE BETRAYED GUARDIAN and PEY KARUPPI. Do not use either as the current story title or active project identity.
- The titles were retired because they imply a direct identity or mythology association that the story has deliberately avoided establishing.
- This is a naming correction only. Existing story material and approved decisions are preserved. No new mythology or replacement premise is implied.
- The GitHub repository slug and historical research IDs remain unchanged for archival continuity. Legacy book titles, research labels, folder names and source references are not automatically renamed.
- The final title remains open and must emerge from the story's own identity.

## Project boundary

- Preserve the present-day **1990** village mystery as currently established.
- The former historical **Story B** mythology has been removed from active development.
- No replacement mythology is approved.
- Do not invent causal links between separate current-day mysteries.
- Nothing becomes story canon merely because it appears in a research note.

The study distinguishes reported belief, witness chains, inquisitorial framing, notarial mediation and historical inference, while preserving Ginzburg's explicit uncertainty about the physical occurrence of the reported night gatherings. Conditional transfer note archived; no canon changes.
- **INTL-004 — *The Terror That Comes in the Night***: full-book study of the supplied 235-page English PDF by David J. Hufford (University of Pennsylvania Press, 1982). The experience-centered approach, Cultural Source / Experiential Source hypotheses, Newfoundland narratives, questionnaire design, Old Hag phenomenology, sleep-literature critique, cross-cultural examples, conclusion and feature index are mapped. The study distinguishes reported experience, cultural interpretation and causal explanation, and preserves the author's refusal to settle the supernatural question. Conditional transfer note archived; no canon changes.
- **INTL-005 — *The Ghost Bride***: full-book study of the supplied 372-page English PDF by Yangsze Choo (copyright 2013; supplied HarperCollins first-edition digital text). All 40 chapters across four parts, plus the author's notes and copyright material, are mapped. The study separates the fictional ghost-marriage plot and invented afterlife from the author's explanatory cultural notes, and reconstructs the Lim Tian Ching death mystery, underworld conspiracy, character network, object clues and supernatural rules. Conditional KARUPPI transfer note archived; no canon changes.
- **PSYCH-001 — *The Righteous Mind***: detailed study of Jonathan Haidt's 2012 book based on all 12 substantive chapters, introduction and conclusion of the supplied 458-page PDF. The dossier maps the rider / elephant model, social intuitionist reasoning, reputation and accountability, WEIRD moral-cultural variation, the six moral foundations, groupishness, collective ritual, religion as believing / doing / belonging, moral systems and moral capital. A separate KARUPPI transfer note provides character and conflict worksheets without changing canon. The end-matter bibliography and notes were not independently audited reference by reference.
- **PSYCH-002 — *Man's Search for Meaning***: detailed study of the supplied 136-page Beacon Press English edition by Viktor E. Frankl. The study maps the three psychological phases described in the concentration-camp account, love and inner life, the role of future goals, responsibility, the will to meaning, existential frustration, the existential vacuum, the three routes to meaning, logotherapy's techniques, and tragic optimism. It distinguishes Frankl's memoir, theory, foreword and afterword, and explicitly warns against romanticising suffering or turning limited inner freedom into victim-blame. Conditional KARUPPI transfer note archived; no canon changes.

## Evidence labels

- **SOURCE** — what the source demonstrably contains.
- **ANALYSIS** — our interpretation of why or how it works.
- **APPLICATION** — a possible technique for the story project; not canon unless explicitly approved.
- **OPEN / NEEDS VERIFICATION** — incomplete, uncertain, or requiring a primary-source check.

## Working principles

1. Challenge, don't flatter. Keep only techniques that improve the story.
2. No invented page numbers, timestamps, quotations, or bibliographic details.
3. Do not upload full copyrighted books or film materials. Store original source files outside the repository.
4. Study one work at a time and select the next source based on a specific craft question.
5. When a scan is poor, stop rather than manufacturing certainty from OCR noise.

Start with `00_MASTER_INDEX/PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `00_MASTER_INDEX/RESEARCH_REGISTER.md`, and `00_MASTER_INDEX/BOOK_ACQUISITION_SHORTLIST.md`.
