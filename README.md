# KARUPPI — Research Archive

A version-controlled, evidence-led research library for **KARUPPI: The Betrayed Guardian**, an animated Tamil folklore-horror mystery film.

## Project boundary

- Preserve the present-day **1990** village mystery as currently established.
- The former historical **Story B** mythology has been removed from active development.
- No replacement mythology is approved.
- Do not invent causal links between separate current-day mysteries.
- Nothing becomes story canon merely because it appears in a research note.

## Research represented

- **ISR-001 — *Ragasiyamaaga Oru Ragasiyam***: deep study synthesis from prior page-image / OCR-assisted review; page-level audit pending.
- **ISR-002 — *Vittu Vidu Karuppa!***: previously read / extracted and discussed; expanded high-level study included. This is not claimed to be a fresh chapter-by-chapter audit.
- **ISR-003 — *Thangakkaadu***: scan / OCR blocked; no plot study claimed.
- **ISR-004 — *Adhu Mattum Ragasiyam***: scanned-source study covering the opening, investigation, historical account, shrine revelation, and final confession. Tamil OCR was used as a navigation aid and critical pages were visually checked. The supplied scan appears to omit printed pages 2–5.
- **ISR-005 — *Aranmanai Ragasiyam 1***: full-arc study of the supplied 74-page scan (approximately 144 printed pages). Critical reveals and ending were visually checked. The supplied copy's Munnovel / Sulekhar Printers colophon differs from the external catalogue's publisher data.
- **ISR-006 — *Kallukkul Pugundha Uyir***: full-arc study of the supplied 74-page PDF, covering all 11 chapters, the Naganatha / Nandana / Nandi threads, Pallava-era sculptural story, rare animation, and ending. OCR was used as navigation support; plot-critical material was checked against page images. Edition metadata is not established by the supplied scan.
- **ISR-007 — *Abaya Vanam***: first-pass full-arc and 38-chapter structure study of the supplied 320-page PDF. The Tamil text layer is corrupted, so rendered page images were used for chapter openings, endings, selected intervening passages, and the final sequence. The study separates Siddha / samadhi / sacred-grove / nakshatra-tree context from the novel's specific extraordinary claims. A conditional transfer note is archived; neither note changes story canon.
- **ISR-008 — *Chidambara Ragasiyam***: first-pass full-structure study of the supplied 541-page PDF, mapping 90 numbered sections and targeted page-image reading. The Tamil text layer is corrupted, but the scan is visually clear. The work is a Chidambaram temple reference compilation, not a story collection. Historical claims are checked against ASI, academic and institutional sources; a conditional transfer note is archived with no canon changes.
- **INTL-001 — *The Inugami Curse***: full-arc study of the supplied 246-page English PDF (translated by Yumiko Yamazaki). The study maps the inheritance mechanism, family relationships, three-heirloom curse structure, identity swaps, clue chain, murders and final unresolved poison detail. It separates the novel's fictional curse from documented Japanese inugami-possession traditions. A conditional KARUPPI transfer note is archived; no canon changes.
- **INTL-002 — *Folk***: full-book study of the supplied 155-page English PDF. All 15 interconnected stories, the Neverness map, recurring character network, community belief systems, sensory grammar, seasonal structure and circular Prick Song / Tether frame are mapped. The end matter is checked for edition metadata and the author's Isle of Man acknowledgement. A conditional KARUPPI transfer note is archived; no canon changes.
- **INTL-003 — *The Night Battles***: full study of the supplied English edition of Carlo Ginzburg's *I Benandanti*. The four chapters, trial-record appendix, translators' note, prefaces and visual plates are reviewed. The study distinguishes reported belief, witness chains, inquisitorial framing, notarial mediation and historical inference, while preserving Ginzburg's explicit uncertainty about the physical occurrence of the reported night gatherings. Conditional transfer note archived; no canon changes.
- **INTL-004 — *The Terror That Comes in the Night***: full-book study of the supplied 235-page English PDF by David J. Hufford (University of Pennsylvania Press, 1982). The experience-centered approach, Cultural Source / Experiential Source hypotheses, Newfoundland narratives, questionnaire design, Old Hag phenomenology, sleep-literature critique, cross-cultural examples, conclusion and feature index are mapped. The study distinguishes reported experience, cultural interpretation and causal explanation, and preserves the author's refusal to settle the supernatural question. Conditional transfer note archived; no canon changes.
- **INTL-005 — *The Ghost Bride***: full-book study of the supplied 372-page English PDF by Yangsze Choo (copyright 2013; supplied HarperCollins first-edition digital text). All 40 chapters across four parts, plus the author's notes and copyright material, are mapped. The study separates the fictional ghost-marriage plot and invented afterlife from the author's explanatory cultural notes, and reconstructs the Lim Tian Ching death mystery, underworld conspiracy, character network, object clues and supernatural rules. Conditional KARUPPI transfer note archived; no canon changes.
- **FILM-001 — *Rosemary's Baby***: selected sensory-grammar observations, not a complete film study.
- **CAT-001 — Indra Soundar Rajan catalogue screen**: preliminary relevance-ranked shortlist from a 196-entry author catalogue. Two user-provided screenshots of the page-3 Most-read grid have been visually screened; some cover titles remain uncertain.

## International research programme

The five-book international sequence is complete (5/5): INTL-001 (*The Inugami Curse*), INTL-002 (*Folk*), INTL-003 (*The Night Battles*), INTL-004 (*The Terror That Comes in the Night*) and INTL-005 (*The Ghost Bride*). The next step is a five-book comparative synthesis, followed by the separate 20-film international research programme.

## Evidence labels

- **SOURCE** — what the source demonstrably contains.
- **ANALYSIS** — our interpretation of why or how it works.
- **APPLICATION** — a possible technique for KARUPPI; not canon unless explicitly approved.
- **OPEN / NEEDS VERIFICATION** — incomplete, uncertain, or requiring a primary-source check.

## Working principles

1. Challenge, don't flatter. Keep only techniques that improve the story.
2. No invented page numbers, timestamps, quotations, or bibliographic details.
3. Do not upload full copyrighted books or film materials. Store original source files outside the repository.
4. Study one work at a time and select the next source based on a specific craft question.
5. When a scan is poor, stop rather than manufacturing certainty from OCR noise.

Start with `00_MASTER_INDEX/PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `00_MASTER_INDEX/RESEARCH_REGISTER.md`, and `00_MASTER_INDEX/BOOK_ACQUISITION_SHORTLIST.md`.
