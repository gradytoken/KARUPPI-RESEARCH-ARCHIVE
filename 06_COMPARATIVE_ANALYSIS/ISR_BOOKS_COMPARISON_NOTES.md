## 1. What the preserved notes support

| Dimension | *Vittu Vidu Karuppa!* | *Ragasiyamaaga Oru Ragasiyam* |
|---|---|---|
| Opening disturbance | Folk verse / incantatory register followed by an impossible temple-bell sound at midnight on a full-moon night. | A village temple's sunset-closing rule and the reported consequences of violating it form a central mystery engine. |
| Community | The village collectively reacts to the disturbance; local discussion helps establish what is considered abnormal. | Different characters approach the temple through belief, rationalism, medicine, and inherited knowledge. |
| Geography | Temple, banyan tree, stone platform, burial-ground edge, fields, forest, and mountain-side geography are present in the preserved notes. | Mountain settlement, temple, spring, forest paths, ashram, cave, and routes towards the foothills structure the investigation. |
| Information carriers | Folk verse / oral memory, physical places, and objects are noted as story-bearing elements. | Rule, inscription, spring, palm-leaf messages, physical access, hidden cave, and later revelations are noted. |
| Causality | Preserved notes identify human deception, treasure, village power, and supernatural justice as layered registers. | A human criminal operation explains part of the apparent supernatural activity, while a spiritual layer remains in the fictional world. |

## 2. Cautions

This table compares only what survived in the prior research notes. It does not prove that either novel is superior, more coherent, or more effective overall. Equivalent scene logs, complete character maps, and page-level citations are not yet available for both books.

## 3. Craft questions for a later full comparison

1. How early does each novel establish a rule or disturbance that the audience can observe?
2. How does each distribute knowledge between protagonist, community, and audience?
3. Which clues change decisions rather than simply add atmosphere?
4. How do physical locations control access, risk, and information?
5. Does the ending reinterpret earlier evidence fairly?
6. Does a human explanation close the mystery too completely, or preserve a meaningful residue?

## 4. Conditional KARUPPI application — questions, not story decisions
