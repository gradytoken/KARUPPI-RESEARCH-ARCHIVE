The active project is an animated Tamil folklore-horror mystery film set in an isolated Tamil village with mountain / forest geography. The present-day setup is the material to preserve while research continues. Mysteries are not to be explained, linked, or reinterpreted without evidence from the latest Master Story Bible and an explicit creative decision.

Build a rigorous research library. Book studies proceed one source at a time. The international film programme proceeds two films per comparative pair, with scene-level timestamps, evidence chains, folklore and culture boundaries, psychology, atmosphere, sound, visual grammar, mystery architecture, narrative consequences, and audience-pulse research. The archive currently includes studies / notes for *Vittu Vidu Karuppa!*, *Ragasiyamaaga Oru Ragasiyam*, *Adhu Mattum Ragasiyam*, *Aranmanai Ragasiyam 1*, *Kallukkul Pugundha Uyir*, *Abaya Vanam* and *Chidambara Ragasiyam*, plus all five international book studies, INTL-001 (*The Inugami Curse*), INTL-002 (*Folk*), INTL-003 (*The Night Battles*), INTL-004 (*The Terror That Comes in the Night*) and INTL-005 (*The Ghost Bride*). *Thangakkaadu* remains blocked, and *Rosemary's Baby* is represented by selected sensory observations. Conditional craft-transfer notes are research applications, not story decisions. PSYCH-001 (*The Righteous Mind*) adds moral psychology; PSYCH-002 (*Man's Search for Meaning*) adds existential motivation, responsibility and meaning under pressure. Both have conditional character-conflict worksheets and neither changes story canon.

Current craft questions include village life and collective behaviour, rules and consequences, mystery information flow, geography as a suspense mechanism, sensory motifs, conflicting belief systems, clue progression, the coexistence of human deception with uncanny evidence, and the relationship between forest ecology, local belief, and practical knowledge.

Do not start a screenplay, rebuild the mythology, or produce new plot explanations unless explicitly requested.

## Decision rules

- Challenge ideas that are clichéd, decorative, derivative, over-explanatory, or unsupported.
- Keep source observations separate from interpretation and story suggestions.
- Preserve uncertainty. Repetition does not turn a hypothesis into a fact.
- Consult the latest Master Story Bible before changing the status of a current-day element.
- Complete book studies one source at a time. Conduct the international film programme in two-film pairs, completing the comparative dossier and GitHub register update before proceeding to the next pair.

## Canon status vocabulary

- **LOCKED** — explicitly approved by the user or clearly fixed in the latest master bible.
- **ACTIVE WORKING ELEMENT** — currently used in the setup, but details may need source verification.
- **OPEN MYSTERY** — intentionally unresolved.
- **PARKED / REMOVED** — not active development material.
- **PROPOSAL** — an idea only; not canon.
- **NEEDS SOURCE CHECK** — referenced in prior discussion but must be checked against the latest source document.
