## PSYCH-001 — The Righteous Mind

**Study:** 02_BOOK_STUDIES/PSYCH_001_The_Righteous_Mind.md  
**Transfer note:** 07_KARUPPI_APPLICATION/PSYCH_001_THE_RIGHTEOUS_MIND_TRANSFER_NOTE.md

The supplied 458-page English PDF (Pantheon Books, 2012) was studied across all 12 substantive chapters, introduction and conclusion. The dossier covers:

- Rider / elephant model and social intuitionist reasoning.
- Moral dumbfounding, intuition and post hoc justification.
- Reputation, accountability and the inner lawyer / press secretary.
- WEIRD cultural psychology and the ethics of autonomy, community and divinity.
- The six moral foundations: Care, Fairness, Loyalty, Authority, Sanctity and Liberty.
- The transition from the early five-foundation version to the later six-foundation model.
- Groupishness, shared intentionality and the contested multilevel-selection argument.
- Hive switch, synchrony, ritual and collective effervescence.
- Religion as believing, doing and belonging.
- Moral systems, moral capital and the way cohesion can bind and blind.

The source study distinguishes Haidt's claims from story applications. It explicitly qualifies his American political findings, the limits of cross-cultural generalisation, and his own uncertainty about group selection. The end-matter bibliography and notes were not independently audited reference by reference.

No KARUPPI canon changes were made.
