## PSYCH-001 — The Righteous Mind

**Study:** 02_BOOK_STUDIES/PSYCH_001_The_Righteous_Mind.md  
**Transfer note:** 07_KARUPPI_APPLICATION/PSYCH_001_THE_RIGHTEOUS_MIND_TRANSFER_NOTE.md

The supplied 458-page English PDF (Pantheon Books, 2012) was studied across all 12 substantive chapters, introduction and conclusion. The dossier covers:

- Rider / elephant model and social intuitionist reasoning.
- Moral dumbfounding, intuition and post hoc justification.
- Reputation, accountability and the inner lawyer / press secretary.
- WEIRD cultural psychology and the ethics of autonomy, community and divinity.
- The six moral foundations: Care, Fairness, Loyalty, Authority, Sanctity and Liberty.
- The transition from the early five-foundation version to the later six-foundation model.
- Groupishness, shared intentionality and the contested multilevel-selection argument.
- Hive switch, synchrony, ritual and collective effervescence.
- Religion as believing, doing and belonging.
- Moral systems, moral capital and the way cohesion can bind and blind.

The source study distinguishes Haidt's claims from story applications. It explicitly qualifies his American political findings, the limits of cross-cultural generalisation, and his own uncertainty about group selection. The end-matter bibliography and notes were not independently audited reference by reference.

## PSYCH-002 — Man's Search for Meaning

**Study:** 02_BOOK_STUDIES/PSYCH_002_Mans_Search_for_Meaning.md  
**Transfer note:** 07_KARUPPI_APPLICATION/PSYCH_002_MANS_SEARCH_FOR_MEANING_TRANSFER_NOTE.md

The supplied 136-page English PDF was reviewed through its complete structure: Kushner foreword, Frankl's 1992 preface, Part One, Part Two, the 1984 postscript, Winslade afterword and copyright material. The dossier covers:

- The three psychological phases Frankl describes: shock, apathy and the post-liberation response.
- The effect of deprivation, hunger, uncertainty, dehumanization and loss of future orientation.
- Love, memory, nature, art, humour, solitude and inner life under coercive conditions.
- The limited freedom to take a stand within conditions, and the inseparable role of responsibility.
- The will to meaning, existential frustration and the existential vacuum.
- Three routes to meaning: creative work or deeds, experience and love, and attitude toward unavoidable suffering.
- Logotherapy, self-transcendence, hyper-intention, hyper-reflection, dereflection and paradoxical intention.
- The tragic triad of pain, guilt and death, and Frankl's account of tragic optimism.

The dossier distinguishes Frankl's memoir, theory, foreword and afterword. It treats the camp narrative as a first-person survivor account rather than representative epidemiology, and does not present the clinical examples as current treatment guidance. It emphasizes Frankl's explicit warning against glorifying or manufacturing suffering.

No KARUPPI canon changes were made.
