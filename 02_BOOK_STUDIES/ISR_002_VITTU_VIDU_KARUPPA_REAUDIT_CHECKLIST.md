# *Vittu Vidu Karuppa!* — Future Re-audit Checklist

This checklist prevents the current high-level synthesis from being mistaken for a complete critical edition.

## Required pass

- [ ] Record chapter boundaries and page ranges from the actual edition.
- [ ] Build a chapter-by-chapter event sequence.
- [ ] Identify protagonist, opposing forces, supporting characters, and their functions.
- [ ] Track the folk verse / incantatory frame: first use, recurrence, reinterpretation, and payoff.
- [ ] Track the midnight temple-bell event and every later reference to it.
- [ ] Map village geography and verify spatial relationships.
- [ ] Log collective behaviour and changes in community response.
- [ ] Track each physical clue and what action it changes.
- [ ] Separate human deception, village power, treasure, and supernatural-justice threads.
- [ ] Record the exact reveal sequence and final explanation.
- [ ] Evaluate whether the ending pays off the opening's promise.
- [ ] Add page-specific evidence for every source claim.
- [ ] Only then write a complete strengths / weaknesses assessment.

## Scene-log format

| Chapter / page | Observable event | Character knowledge | Audience knowledge | New clue / rule | Decision or consequence | Evidence confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pending | Pending | Pending | Pending | Pending | Pending | Needs source review |

## Do not do

- Do not invent a synopsis to fill missing notes.
- Do not infer the ending from the genre or title.
- Do not quote OCR output without checking the page image.
- Do not state that a device works throughout the novel based only on its opening.
